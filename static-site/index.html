Our story

Born with the new century

Shatabdi means "century." The school was formally inaugurated on 26 October 2000 by Janab Saiyid Hamid, former Vice-Chancellor of Aligarh Muslim University and then Chancellor of Jamia Hamdard.

It was the first venture of the Shatabdi Foundation, a society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. CBSE first affiliated the school for the secondary classes and later upgraded it to Senior Secondary (Class XII). Its current affiliation runs to 2030.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which board is the school affiliated to?

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, at Senior Secondary level. Affiliation No. 330113, valid 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2030.

Which classes do you offer?

Pre-primary (PP-1, PP-2) through Class XII. Classes XI–XII offer Science (with or without Mathematics) and Commerce.

When do admissions open?

Admissions for Std I to IX usually open in December. Admission is based on a written entrance test and an interview. See the Admissions page for details.

Is the school co-educational?

Yes. Shatabdi Public School is a co-educational, English-medium day school. There is no hostel.

Is school transport available?

Yes, school transport is available. Please contact the office for routes and charges.

Where is the school located?

Katari Hill Road, Aliganj, Gaya, Bihar 823001, near Station Road.
