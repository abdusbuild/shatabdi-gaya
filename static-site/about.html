About the school

Twenty-five years of English-medium CBSE education on Katari Hill Road, built on togetherness and service.

Who we are

A Shatabdi Foundation school

Shatabdi Public School is an English-medium, co-educational senior secondary school affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi. It is run by the Shatabdi Foundation, a society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

The Foundation started the school as its first venture. Janab Saiyid Hamid, former Vice-Chancellor of Aligarh Muslim University and then Chancellor of Jamia Hamdard, formally inaugurated it on 26 October 2000. The school now teaches children from pre-primary to Class XII.

Our mission

To promote togetherness and service to humanity, and to strengthen the Ganga-Jamuni Hindustani tahzeeb, our shared cultural heritage.

Our vision

To shape confident, compassionate young people who blend modern knowledge with strong moral values, and who carry the spirit of the new century into their communities.

Our values

Discipline, honesty, respect for every faith and culture, and admission open to all students regardless of caste, creed or religion.

Our journey

From a new society's first project to a Senior Secondary school with a campus of more than two and a half acres.
