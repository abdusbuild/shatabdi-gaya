Facilities

Everything a learner needs

Science labs

Separate Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories for practical work from the middle school onwards.

Computer lab

A computer lab supports IT and Multimedia & Web Technology classes.

Library

A library with reference books, fiction and periodicals that encourages the reading habit.

Smart classrooms

Interactive boards and audio-visual aids in our classrooms bring lessons to life.

Playground

Open grounds for cricket, football, volleyball, kabaddi, kho-kho and athletics.

School transport

School buses cover routes across Gaya. Contact the office for routes and fares.

Safe campus

A boundary wall, fencing, CCTV and security staff keep the campus secure.

First aid

First-aid support is available on campus throughout the school day.

Arts & activity rooms

Space for art, music, dance and dramatics, and an auditorium for assemblies and events.

House system

Four houses, one school

Every student belongs to one of four houses, named after India's great rivers, and competes in sports, quizzes and cultural events throughout the year.
