Academics

A complete CBSE pathway, from a child's first day in pre-primary to the Class XII board examinations.

Stages of learning

From PP-1 to Class XII

The academic session runs from April to March, with summer vacation from mid-May to mid-June.

PP-1 · PP-2

Pre-primary

Play-based learning that builds language, number sense, motor skills and confidence. The minimum entry age is 4 years.

Class I – V

Primary

Strong foundations in English, Hindi, Mathematics and Environmental Studies, with art, music and games.

Class VI – VIII

Middle

Science, Social Science, languages and computer education, with more hands-on lab work.

Class IX – XII

Secondary & Senior Secondary

Preparation for the CBSE board examinations, with Science and Commerce streams in Classes XI and XII.

Class X

Secondary scheme of studies

Class X students study five subjects plus one compulsory vocational subject, with the option of a sixth subject, as the CBSE scheme prescribes.

  • English and a second language (Hindi or Urdu)
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Science
  • A vocational or skill subject, such as Information Technology
