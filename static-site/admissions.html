Admissions

Admission is open to every child, regardless of caste, creed or religion. Here is how to join Shatabdi.

How it works

Admission process

Admissions for Std I to IX usually open in December for the session that begins in April. Pre-primary admissions (PP-1, PP-2) need a minimum age of 4 years.

Collect & submit the form

Get the admission form (and the transport form, if needed) from the school office and submit it with the registration fee.

Entrance test

A 50-mark written test in English, Mathematics, Science and General Knowledge, based on the previous class syllabus.

Interview

Shortlisted students and their parents meet the school for an interaction. Candidates need at least 40% in English, Maths, Science and Hindi in their last examination.

Confirm admission

Deposit the fees within 15 days of the test result being declared, or the seat may be offered to another candidate.

Checklist

Documents required

Please bring these at the time of admission.

  • Completed admission form (and transport form, if needed)
  • 3 photographs of the child with both parents
  • 3 passport-size photographs of the child
  • Photocopy of the last examination mark sheet
  • Original birth certificate
  • Original transfer certificate (TC) from the previous school
  • Proof of residence
  • Proof of parents' occupation
Session

April – March

The academic year begins in April. Summer vacation runs from mid-May to mid-June.

Questions

Admission FAQs

What is the minimum age for admission?

Children must be at least 4 years old for entry to pre-primary.

Is there an entrance test for every class?

Admission to Std I to IX is through a 50-mark entrance test and an interview. For pre-primary and Class XI, please check the process with the office.

When must the fees be paid?

Fees must be deposited within 15 days of the entrance test result.

Does the school have a hostel?

No. Shatabdi Public School is a day school.

Which languages are taught?

English is the medium of instruction. Hindi and Urdu are offered as languages.
